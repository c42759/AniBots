## Overview
Raya Ato is Kura's warm, resilient mother. Following her husband's tragic and mysterious death 4 years prior (3043 AD), Raya has fought tirelessly to keep the Ato Family Workshop open, managing inventory accounts and taking on customer relations while Kura handles physical repairs.

## Role in Narrative
Raya anchors Kura's emotional world. She guards her late husband's encrypted research notebooks and historical records regarding the Generation 0 project and Vault 7. As Kura progresses through his journey, Raya helps decipher key passwords and unlocks family heirlooms.

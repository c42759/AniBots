## Overview
Isabell Tirio (often called "Isa") is a 15-year-old student living in Neo Metropolis and the younger sister of Luccas "Luk" Tirio. Standing at 160 cm, she is a bright, kind-hearted, and energetic presence who adores her older brother and supports his competitive AniBot ambitions unconditionally. While naturally sweet and enthusiastic, she gets noticeably flustered and shy whenever Kura Ato is around the workshop.

## Personality
Cheerful, kind, and deeply affectionate with family and friends. Isa possesses natural curiosity and infectious energy paired with quiet determination. Though modest about her own abilities, her loyalty and courage shine brightest when cheering on Luk and her friends.

## Dream & Aspirations
Isa dreams of finding her own distinct path in the fast-paced world of 3047 AD, creating unforgettable memories with those she cherishes, and ultimately standing proudly beside her big brother as an accomplished partner and handler.

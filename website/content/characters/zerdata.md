## Overview
Zerdata is Kura Ato's partner AniBot, built around the legendary **Vulpes Zerda** Ancient Series core (`G0-004`). Left anonymously on Kura's bedroom window sill by an unknown Ancient AniBot, Zerdata awoke to bond with Kura and form an unbeatable partnership.

## Design Motif & Appearance
- **Motif**: Fennec Fox (*Vulpes Zerda*) equipped with oversized acoustic radar dish ears.
- **Color Palette**: Primary Vibrant Orange (`#FFA500`), Secondary Clean White & Dark Slate Gray armor, glowing Cyan diode eyes.
- **Silhouette**: Agile, spring-loaded 120 cm chassis built for rapid sidestepping and acoustic resonance.

## Modular Chassis Anatomy
- **Head**: Acoustic Dish Ears (`part_head_zerda_radar_ears`) — Micro-vibration acoustic radar array.
- **Torso**: Echo-Sync Chassis (`part_torso_zerda_core`) — Heat dispersion ducts & engram sync unit.
- **Left Arm**: Sonic Pulse Cannon (`part_arm_l_sonic_emitter`) — Focused acoustic shockwave emitter.
- **Right Arm**: Resonant Strike Claw (`part_arm_r_resonator_claw`) — High-frequency parrying claw.
- **Legs**: Vulpine Dash Sprinters (`part_legs_zerda_springers`) — Agility springers for high evasion.

## Combat Abilities
- **Echo Counter**: Detects high-frequency motor vibrations (+30% Evasion and automatic counter-attack on dodged strikes).
- **Acoustic Step**: Dodges incoming attack and strikes target structural weak point.
- **Zerda Apex Burst**: Executes 4 rapid sonic strikes while remaining immune to incoming damage.

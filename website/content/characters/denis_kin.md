## Overview
Denis "Nis" Kin is a 14-year-old AniBot handler living in Neo Metropolis and the older brother of Martinn "Tim" Kin. Thoughtful, observant, and tech-curious, Denis serves as the tactical anchor of the brothers' doubles team. While the two brothers excel when sparring against one another, their early doubles matches frequently dissolved into chaos—both brothers chasing the same openings, colliding in mid-ring, and damaging the sides of their AniBots that faced each other. After becoming repeat customers at the Ato Family Workshop to repair scraped shield mounts, Kura Ato and Luk Tirio took Denis and Tim under their wing to teach them true team coordination, lane discipline, and mutual protection.

## Personality & Tactical Mindset
Denis is analytical, calm, and protective of his younger brother, often bearing an older-sibling weariness with a fond smile. Rather than seeking flashy solo knockouts, he focuses on spatial awareness, positional coverage, and timing. He loves gaming, technology blueprints, and tinkering with team comms devices. Through their coaching sessions with Kura, Luk, and Sammy's Trouble Crew, Denis learned to stop over-planning every step for his brother and instead communicate clear intent while trusting Tim's instincts.

## Overview
Spy is a 16-year-old member of District 4's Trouble Crew, acting as Sammy's scout and messenger. Constantly scurrying across alley rooftops, fire escapes, and scrap yards, Spy loves trying to play the part of a shadowy informant. In reality, he is rather innocent, gullible, and downright clumsy at times, frequently misinterpreting the rumors he overhears or spilling his own secrets by talking too much.

## Personality
Curious, excitable, innocent, and often foolish. Spy genuinely thinks he is a master of espionage, but his goofy demeanor and loose tongue give him away instantly. Despite being easily duped and constantly bossed around by Sammy, he is loyal to his buddies and tags along with whatever mischief the crew cooks up.

## Quirks & Habits
- **Information Overload**: Eagerly tries to gather dirt on everyone in District 4, but often gets key facts completely mixed up.
- **Motor-Mouth**: Starts rambling uncontrollably whenever he gets excited or nervous.
- **Blind Follower**: Follows Sammy's bullying schemes without really thinking about the consequences.

## Field Gear & Appearance
- **Scout Goggles**: Yellow-tinted visor propped on his hair or neck, worn mostly because he thinks it looks cool and high-tech.
- **Handheld Scanner / Communicator**: Flip-gadget terminal he uses to track scrap shipments and snoop on local frequencies.
- **Layered D4 Hoodie**: Oversized black and yellow windbreaker jacket emblazoned with the District 4 emblem.
- **Sling Bag**: Bulging with loose cables, half-broken sensors, and street snacks.

## Overview
Anda Tal is a top-ranking academic student in Neo Metropolis. Unlike Kura, who balances freelance salvage work and mechanic repairs to keep his household afloat, Anda is exclusively focused on academic and competitive excellence ("Student Only"). He rejects sentimental camaraderie in favor of relentless performance, disciplined preparation, and technical perfection.

## Visual Design & Streetwear Style
- **Hair & Eyes**: Layered ash-silver hair, piercing slate-blue/silver eyes.
- **Attire**: Modern academic streetwear uniform — tailored sleeveless vest with gold-embroidered crest, loosened dark necktie, short-sleeve collared shirt, dark relaxed utility slacks with hip straps, high-top tech-sneakers.
- **Accessories**: Fingerless gloves with reinforced wrist cuffs and crest emblem; two-tone cap (worn casually forward or backward).
- **Color Palette**: Charcoal Black (`#1E1E24`), Slate Gray (`#4A4E69`), Off-White (`#E5E5E5`), Muted Ochre (`#C68B59`).

## Dynamic with Kura Ato
Anda represents the disciplined, analytical academy counterpart to Kura's street-smart scrap mechanic background. He serves as an intellectual benchmark rival whom Kura must outthink rather than overpower.

## Partner Machine: Ancient-Wyrm

- **Core Chip**: `chip_draco` (Astral Overclock Matrix)
- **Combat Style**: Draconic overclock surges, high-precision thermal beams, and rapid aerial positioning.

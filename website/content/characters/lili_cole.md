## Overview
Lili Cole is a 72-year-old mountain innkeeper nestled high above District 2 in Neo Metropolis. While the valleys of District 2 buzz with hyper-competitive research academies and elite handler training facilities, Lili operates a tranquil mountain retreat known as **Mountain Springs Haven**. Her small hotel is celebrated for its natural geothermal hot springs, therapeutic mineral baths, and soothing hospitality that draws fatigued academy students, veteran handlers, and mountain trekkers alike.

## Personality & Background
Gentle, perceptive, and unfailingly warm, Lili is an affectionate maternal figure to anyone stepping through her inn's rustic cedar doors. Having spent decades witnessing the rapid technological growth of Neo Metropolis from her mountain vantage point, she provides grounded perspective to young handlers blinded by competitive pressure. Beneath her sweet demeanor lies an experienced mountain guide who knows every winding alpine pass and hidden thermal spring in District 2.

## Mountain Springs Haven (The Thermal Inn)
- **Geothermal Baths**: Natural thermal mineral waters heated by subterranean volcanic veins, renowned for easing mechanical stress and restoring handler focus.
- **Rustic Hospitality**: Organic mountain herbal teas, home-cooked regional meals, and peaceful tatami-lined quarters overlooking the northern skyline.
- **Neutral Ground**: A serene sanctuary where fierce academic rivals and weary wanderers leave their rivalries at the entryway.

## Signature Appearance & Demeanor
- **Traditional Layered Warmth**: Hand-knit mountain wool shawl draped over a traditional quilted coat and comfortable apron.
- **Silver Wisdom**: Neatly gathered snow-white hair and kind crinkled eyes that radiate decades of peaceful mountain living.
- **Teapot & Herb Pouch**: Frequently seen tending steaming kettles of mountain tea or gathering medicinal alpine herbs.

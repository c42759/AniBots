## Overview
Master Jax is a gruff, master scrapper and retired battle Handler who oversees the massive recycling yard in Neo Metropolis District 7. Having survived dozens of tournaments in his youth, Jax knows every shortcut, illegal modification, and salvage restoration technique in the book.

## Mentorship & Quests
Jax teaches Kura how to restore heavily degraded QC2 and QC3 parts salvaged from battlefields. He offers scavenging contracts, rogue machine bounties, and unlocks advanced blueprint tuning in the Ato Family Workshop.

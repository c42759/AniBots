## Overview
Vera "Viper" Lin is Magnus's top enforcer and the ruthless ringmaster of **The Pit**, the most notorious illegal underground combat circuit in Rust-Ridge. Sharp-tongued, observant, and deadly with chemical weaponry, Vera weeds out weak contenders and recruits desperate talent for the syndicate.

## Quest & Faction Role
In the story's branching paths, Handlers who venture into the Badlands must face Vera's gauntlet to gain audience with Magnus or uncover syndicate smuggling manifests.

## Partner Machine: Serpent-Coil
- **Chassis**: `anibot_serpent_coil` (Primal Series `ANA-01`)
- **Core Chip**: `chip_corrosive` (Kinetic Acid Controller)
- **Combat Style**: Constriction grapples, neuro-toxic chemical sprays, and terrain-ignoring slither mobility.

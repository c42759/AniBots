## Overview
Slow is a 16-year-old resident of Neo Metropolis District 4 and the heavy muscle of Sammy's Trouble Crew. Big, sturdy, and sleepy-eyed, Slow lumbers along at his own relaxed pace. While not the brightest kid on the block, his colossal physical strength provides all the intimidation Sammy needs when shaking down younger handlers in back alleys.

## Personality
Calm, laid-back, simple-minded, and surprisingly gentle. Slow rarely gets angry and doesn't hold personal grudges, but he does whatever Sammy tells him without questioning it. He believes in sticking by his friends through thick and thin, even when their antics get them into hot water with older district mechanics.

## Quotes & Motto
- *"Same pace. Same people. Always here."*
- *"I got you, no matter the pace."*
- *"Good friends make heavy days lighter."*

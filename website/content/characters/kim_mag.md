## Overview
Kim Mag is a 17-year-old aspiring AniBot trainer hailing from District 6 in southern Neo Metropolis—a bustling industrial zone dominated by freight rail yards, container depots, and distribution warehouses. Unlike handlers backed by corporate sponsorships or elite academies, Kim comes from a modest working-class background with little money to spare. His dream of entering the AniBot circuit began when he uncovered an old, dormant prototype chassis tucked away under tarps in his family garage. Determined to wake the machine, Kim travels to District 4 seeking out Kura Ato, hoping the renowned freelance teenage mechanic can repair and calibrate the vintage bot on a tight budget.

## Personality & Beliefs
Kind, determined, and deeply empathetic, Kim is always looking for ways to make a positive impact on those around him. While the competitive AniBot circuit is often dominated by aggressive rivalry and corporate power plays, Kim sincerely believes in people, genuine friendships, and the latent potential of AniBots to bring a brighter tomorrow to Neo Metropolis. Even when faced with financial hardship or skepticism from wealthier handlers, his warm optimism and stubborn resolve never waver.

## Aspirations & Objectives
1. **Restore the Garage Prototype**: Partner with Kura Ato to diagnose, repair, and recalibrate the vintage AniBot chassis found in his garage.
2. **Earn Official Trainer Accreditation**: Compete in district qualifying bouts to earn recognized trainer status without corporate sponsorship.
3. **District 6 Representation**: Prove that handlers from the freight yards and working-class outer sectors can stand on equal footing with elite academy competitors.
4. **Community Harmony**: Use AniBot sports to foster unity and mutual respect across the segregated districts of Neo Metropolis.

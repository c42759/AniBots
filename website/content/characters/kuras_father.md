## The Unresolved Vanishing of 3043 AD
In **3043 AD**—four years prior to the events of AniBots—Kura's father, **Ty Ato**, mysteriously vanished without a trace. A brilliant freelance robotics engineer and underground investigator, Ty had spent years secretly probing corporate black-market hardware smuggling, suppressed archives from the historic 2750 AD Vault 7 Incident, and the forgotten legacy of the 176 Ancient Series cores.

Despite an official inquiry, authorities never recovered him, his transport, or his remains. While bureaucratic agencies closed their files declaring him presumed lost, Kura and his mother Raya refuse to abandon hope, believing the answers to his disappearance lie locked within corporate networks and Vault 7.

## The Obsolete Chassis Legacy
Before his disappearance, Ty Ato completed the physical assembly of an unpowered AniBot frame hidden within his private workshop study—the chassis that would become **Zerdata** (`anibot_zerdata`).

Unlike sleek, modern commercial AniBots built with lightweight composite alloys and plug-and-play modular interfaces, Ty's machine was deliberately constructed using heavy, obsolete-looking industrial metallurgy, mechanical relays, and unpolished heat-dispersal cowlings. It was severely limited and stubbornly incompatible with modern commercial diagnostic standards and contemporary expectations. It was an intentionally non-standardized machine—built not for corporate sports arenas, but engineered to endure the raw, unthrottled acoustic resonance of an Ancient Series Anima Core.

## Overview
Soren Vance is the golden boy of Aegis Corporation, equipped with bespoke state-of-the-art QC1 commercial frames, private pit crews, and proprietary energy shields. Arrogant, razor-sharp, and disdainful of independent mechanics like Kura, Soren represents the corporate monopoly dominating modern AniBot athletics.

## Tournament Rivalry
Soren serves as the primary corporate antagonist in the Neo Metropolis qualifying tournaments, testing Kura's mechanical ingenuity against corporate-funded overwhelming firepower.

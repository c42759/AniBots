## Overview
Kura is an independent, resourceful 16-year-old living with his mother in Neo Metropolis District 4. His father passed away under mysterious circumstances 4 years prior (3043 AD), leaving behind an unpowered AniBot chassis (`anibot_zerdata`). To support his family, Kura works odd delivery and construction jobs while working as a talented freelance AniBot mechanic for locals.

## Personality & Drive
Highly intelligent, practical, fiercely protective of his family and friends. Knows AniBot hardware inside and out, preferring reliable, well-tuned mechanical balance over corporate-sponsored bloatware.

## Primary Objectives
1. **Support his household**: Help his mother Raya manage the workshop and pay outstanding family debts.
2. **Neighborhood Defense**: Protect street scrappers and novice Handlers from bully syndicates and loan sharks.
3. **The Father's Mystery**: Uncover the truth behind his father's sudden death 4 years ago and the secret data logs left in Vault 7.
4. **World Cup Ambition**: Battle best friend and rival Luccas "Luk" Tirio in the Grand Finals of the AniBot World Cup.

## Partner Machine: Zerdata
- **Chassis**: `anibot_zerdata`
- **Core Chip**: `chip_vulpes_zerda` (Ancient Series `G0-004`)
- **Combat Style**: Acoustic radar tracking, high-evasion sonic counters, and precision disruption strikes.

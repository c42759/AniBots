## Overview
In **2622 AD**, at age 55, independent archaeologist and explorer **Samo Roy** made the single most transformative discovery in human history: the unearthing of crystalline **Anima Stones** within subterranean ruins predating all known civilization. 

Observing that these enigmatic crystals resonated directly with human consciousness, thoughts, and emotional frequencies, Samo bestowed upon them the name *"Anima"*—viewing them not as inert minerals, but as vessels capable of reflecting the human soul.

## Historical Significance
- **2622 AD**: Samo Roy discovers the first Anima Stones across multiple ancient ruins.
- **2640 AD**: Researchers isolate the concentrated **Anima Core** dormant within the raw mineral matrix.
- **2642 AD**: First successful coupling between an Anima Core and electronic circuitry.
- **2650 AD**: Establishment of **Research Center Zero** in Aveiro, Portugal, building upon Roy's expedition field notes.
- **2687 AD**: Construction of **Agon** (`G0-001`), the first AniBot in history.

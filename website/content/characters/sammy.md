## Overview
Sammy is a 16-year-old delinquent living in Neo Metropolis District 4. Reckless, loud, and constantly stirring up trouble, she leads the local street trio known as the **Trouble Crew** alongside her buddies **Spy** and **Slow**. While she and Kura Ato know each other from living in the same district, they are definitely not friends—Sammy is notorious for picking fights, causing headaches for locals, and pushing people around.

## Personality & Habits
Aggressive, energetic, confident, and domineering. Sammy is a district bully who loves throwing her weight around. She frequently corners younger kids, scrapper apprentices, and novice handlers, aggressively coercing them into impromptu AniBot battles just to flaunt her dominance and take what she wants. However, she remains fiercely possessive of her own crew, keeping Spy and Slow tightly under her thumb.

## The Trouble Crew
- **Sammy (Leader & Bully)**: The fiery ringleader picking fights and demanding battles.
- **Spy (The Scout)**: Clumsy informant who runs his mouth, gathers neighborhood gossip, and gets easily confused.
- **Slow (The Muscle)**: Gentle-faced, laid-back powerhouse who backs up Sammy's intimidation purely out of blind loyalty.

## Street Style & Signature Gear
- **Attire**: Street techwear suited for running the backstreets—cropped black tank top with rebel print, distressed teal bomber jacket with neck goggles, and baggy plum cargo pants with trailing utility straps.
- **Signature Accessories**: Chewing gum (constantly blowing pink bubbles), chunky black-and-red runners, red digital wrist timer, and her crew's smiley-face badge.

## Partner Machine: Volt-Pouncer
- **Chassis**: `anibot_volt_pouncer` (Primal Series `TGR-09`)
- **Core Chip**: `chip_spark` (Kinetic Series Spark Core)
- **Combat Style**: Rapid pouncing acrobatics, high-voltage electric discharges, and disruptive Plugtail feints.

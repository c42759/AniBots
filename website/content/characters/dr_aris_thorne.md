## Overview
Born in **2965 AD**, Dr. Aris Thorne is the contemporary elder custodian of the renowned **Thorne Family Scientific Dynasty**—a multi-generational lineage of visionary roboticists spanning nearly three centuries. While his ancestor, Chief Researcher Aris Thorne I, served as lead architect during the historic **Generation 0 Initiative** that culminated in the 176 Ancient Series AniChips in **2750 AD**, the modern Dr. Thorne has dedicated his life to safeguarding the family's classified Vault 7 archives, decrypting harmonic matrix records, and protecting surviving Ancient cores from predatory corporate syndicates.

## Generational Lineage & Historical Timeline
- **2750 AD (The Ancestral Dawn)**: Chief Researcher Dr. Aris Thorne I oversees stabilization trials of raw Anima Stones at Vault 7 / Research Center Zero. When governing authorities schedule Generation 0 for destruction due to fears of uncapped learning abilities, Thorne I secretly bypasses containment protocols, enabling Agon, Biggon, and Cygon to escape with 7 salvaged cores into the Badlands.
- **2800s–2950s (Generational Custodians)**: Across generations, the Thorne family preserves the classified research logs in secret, evading corporate acquisition attempts.
- **2965 AD**: The current Dr. Aris Thorne is born in Neo Metropolis.
- **2995–3020 AD**: Contemporary Dr. Thorne deciphers his family's hereditary frequency journals, identifying lingering acoustic signatures of surviving Ancient cores across the Badlands.
- **3043 AD**: Covertly collaborates with investigator Ty Ato to examine Anode Group black-market Gen-0 scavenging, shortly before Ty Ato's unexplained disappearance.
- **3047 AD (Age 82 — Present)**: Living in secluded scholar exile in Oakhaven Basin, serving as Kura's mentor for Ancient Relic Excavation Quests and harmonic core tuning.

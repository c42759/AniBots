## The Anode Group
- **Leader**: **Magnus**, notorious syndicate boss commanding the Rust-Ridge Badlands black market.
- **Lieutenant**: **Vera "Viper" Lin**, commander of The Pit Arena.
- **Goal**: Expand into civilized eco-cities and seize global control via illegal hardware smuggling, black-market AniChip mods, and unlicensed combat rings.

## The Pit Arena
Located deep within the cracked fissures of Rust-Ridge, **The Pit** operates outside international sporting regulations. Unlike official league matches where part destruction is penalized, The Pit permits salvage claims on defeated frames, making it a lucrative and perilous venue for desperate Handlers.

# What could happen
- Following the confusion in their first practice bout, Kura and Zerdata try short obstacle drills. Kura overcorrects every mistake until Zerdata asks which decisions it is allowed to make. They agree on a small set of clear calls and practise them unsuccessfully before improving.

# What Kura learns
- Clear communication and the first boundaries between Kura’s responsibility and Zerdata’s autonomy.

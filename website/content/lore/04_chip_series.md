## The Landscape of Anima Chip Series

In the 31st century, every AniBot operates under the behavioral and tactical matrix of an **Anima Chip**. These cores are organized into distinct **Series**, representing different epochs of engineering, corporate philosophies, and combat paradigms:

```txt
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       ANIBOTS CHIP SERIES ECOSYSTEM                          │
├─────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. LABORATORY PROTOTYPE │ Ancient Series (Generation 0 — 2750 AD)            │
├─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. AEGIS CORPORATION    │ Antiquity Series (Historical Martial Figures)      │
│                         │ Primal Series (Instinctive Beast Combat)           │
├─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. ATOM CORP            │ Kinetic Series (High-Velocity Energy & Mobility)   │
│                         │ Astral Series (Celestial Precision & Deep Space)   │
├─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. ABFE                 │ Neutral Series (Universal Civic & Balanced Utility)│
│                         │ Pex Series (Modular Expansion & Adaptive Growth)   │
└─────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Ancient Series (Generation 0 — First Project)

![Ancient Series Brand Identity](/images/lore/04_chip_series/series_ancient.jpg)

- **Manufacturer / Origin**: Robotics Research Division (Laboratory Prototype Series)
- **Era & Release**: **2750 AD** (First 176 Prototype Units)
- **Tagline**: *"Built the Future"*
- **Core Philosophy**: Developed in early government and academic laboratory trials directly following the discovery of Anima Stones. Designed without commercial safety limiters, Generation 0 cores feature self-modifying neural architecture, code absorption capabilities, and unmatched overclock headroom.

### Visual Identity & Palette
- **Graphite Black (`#1A1A1A`)**: Primary structure, industrial chassis base.
- **AniBots Red (`#CC0204`)**: Accent identification, signal telemetry.
- **Tech Silver (`#D0D2D4`)**: Machinery surface, unpainted alloy hardware.
- **Lab White (`#F3F3F1`)**: Diagnostic documents, interface cleanroom backgrounds.

### Brand Keywords
`Foundational` • `Prototype` • `Legacy` • `Adaptive` • `Prime Intelligence` • `176 Original Units`

### Canonical AniBot Frames
- **Zerdata (`G0-004`)**: Fennec Fox Acoustic Scout piloted by Kura Ato.
- **Zerdata V2 (`G0-004-B`)**: Overclocked acoustic interceptor frame.
- **Agon (`G0-001`)**: Primordial Vanguard command chassis.
- **Biggon (`G0-002`)**: Immovable Colossus heavy defensive fortress.
- **Cygon (`G0-003`)**: Cybernetic Cipher electronic warfare infiltrator.
- **Kogane (`G0-011`)**: Prototype golden chassis relic.

# What could happen
Kim Mag arrives from District 6 after hearing that a mechanic in District 4 repairs machines other shops dismiss. He brings a rusted AniBot chassis discovered in his storage, hoping it could become his first partner.

Topics and events:
- Kura inspects the chassis with Kim present. Rust has damaged joints, connectors, and exterior fittings, but the torso’s chip housing and supporting structure remain sound. There is no AniChip inside.
- Kim admits that he cannot afford both restoration and a chip. Kura proposes paid delivery work through the workshop, with clear wages and a modest repair plan.
- Kim makes the deliveries himself while the chassis remains unpowered. Between jobs, Kura teaches him basic cleaning, inspection, and maintenance.
- They prioritize safe movement and dependable components over appearance. Kim gradually restores some of the chassis himself.
- Kura helps Kim choose an affordable, compatible modern AniChip. The choice considers the kind of partnership Kim wants, rather than simply the strongest advertised attack.
- Activation introduces a new personality. The awakened AniBot has preferences and questions of its own; Kim must begin getting acquainted with it.
- A gentle practice bout against Zerdata tests movement, response, and safety. Kim becomes overexcited and gives too many commands—echoing Kura’s first practice session.
- Kura stops to explain, lets them try again, and ends the session after a small success rather than pushing for a knockout.

# What Kura learns
Kura’s development: He recognizes his former mistakes in another beginner and can now explain how to avoid them. His expertise becomes visible through patient, practical teaching.

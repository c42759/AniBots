## The Global Landscape

By **3047 AD**, human civilization has undergone radical structural and environmental transformation following centuries of ecological repair. Megacities have evolved into high-density, bio-integrated eco-hybrids: soaring multi-tiered architectural spires wrapped in vertical forests, integrated hydroponic canopies, and automated public transit systems.

Everyday human life is fully networked. Citizens interface with urban infrastructure, logistics networks, and autonomous machines through handheld smart devices and localized holographic terminals. However, this high-tech equilibrium is strictly bounded by hard legal and ethical barriers established during early machine trials.

## Global Demographics & Geopolitics

```txt
                   GLOBAL POPULATION (10 BILLION)
┌──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
│ 4B Eco-City Citizens │ 3B Rural Inhabitants │ 2B Unknown Regions   │ 1B Outlaw Territory  │
│ (Tech & Rule of Law) │ (Traditional/Agrarian│ (Uncharted Frontiers)│ (Gangs & Bandits)    │
└──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

The global population of 10 billion is divided into four distinct geopolitical spheres:

1. **Democratic Eco-Cities (4 Billion)**: High-density urban bastions governed by international law, strict corporate accords, and open-source manufacturing standards. Public life centers around technological equilibrium, clean energy grids, and sanctioned sports leagues like the **AniBot World Cup**.
2. **Rural Agrarian Regions (3 Billion)**: Expansive agricultural basins, river valleys, and traditional communities providing planetary food supplies and localized ecological stewardship.
3. **Unknown Frontier Regions (2 Billion)**: Uncharted wilderness, overgrown ruins from previous eras, and deep isolationist settlements existing outside international sensor coverage.
4. **Outlaw Badlands (1 Billion)**: Desolate wasteland territories governed by criminal cartels, scrap syndicates, and black-market warlords—chief among them the **Anode Group**.

### Forbidden Tech: The Neural Link Ban

Global treaty strictly prohibits direct **Neural Links** between human biological brains and machine kernels. Following early catastrophic experiments that resulted in irreversible cognitive corruption, synaptic burnout, and rogue machine exploits, all machine interaction is legally confined to verbal commands, tactile controllers, and encrypted handheld links.

## The Global Territory Division

![World Map](/images/lore/01_world_setting/world_map_territories.jpg)

Global territory is delineated into two primary operational zones:
- **Blue Dots**: Metropolitan regions where AniBot technology is openly used for work, combat, and leisure.
- **Yellow Dots**: Districts linked to nearby Metropolis.
- **Pink Dots**: Old and New Research Outposts sponsored by AniBot Manufacturing Corporations. Scientific Research.
- **Green Zones (Democratic Accords)**: Stable, rural and suburban areas governed by local laws and regulations. These regions are interconnected by **eco-friendly, high-speed ground transport networks**, monitored maritime corridors, and planetary communication arrays.
- **Orange Zones (Badlands & Wastes)**: Unsanctioned frontier zones where international treaty law does not apply. Here, rogue AniChips, decommissioned military frames, and underground black markets thrive without regulatory oversight.

## The 5 Great Metropolises

### 1. Neo Metropolis (Eurasian Hub)

![Neo Metropolis](/images/lore/01_world_setting/world_map_neo_metropolis.jpg)

The global epicenter of AniBot engineering, commerce, and competitive culture. Built upon a concentric radial footprint around a towering central megastructure, Neo Metropolis spans nine dedicated districts:

- **District 1 (North-Northwest)**: River deltas, freshwater reservoirs, and water filtration networks.
- **District 2 (North-Northeast)**: High-density academic institutions, research academies, and elite handler training facilities (home of prodigy student **Anda Tal**).
- **District 3 (East-Northeast)**: Automated manufacturing centers and certified Quality Control (QC1–QC3) AniChip foundries.
- **District 4 (East)**: Rolling green basins, canal channels, and extensive salvage operations. Houses independent repair garages, scrap depots (**Master Jax**), and the **Ato Family Workshop** (**Kura** and **Raya Ato**).
- **District 5 (Southeast)**: The civic sports capital, featuring modern handler plazas, training gyms, and the **AniBot World Cup Grand Dome**.
- **District 6 (South)**: Heavy logistics rail yards, freight depots, and container distribution warehouses.
- **District 7 (South-Southwest)**: Precision agricultural bio-domes and urban vertical farming towers.
- **District 8 (Southwest)**: Waterfront residential quarters and recreational lake basins.
- **District 9 (West-Northwest)**: Mountainous highlands housing weather radar stations and geothermal sub-stations.

### 2. Adhoc Metropolis (Southern African Coastal Basin)

![Adhoc Metropolis](/images/lore/01_world_setting/world_map_adhoc_metropolis.jpg)

A coastal metropolis situated in Southern Africa, balancing heavy oceanic logistics with nature conservation:

- **Ironbay Docks**: A massive deep-water cargo hub handling oceanic raw material transit and automated container freighters.
- **Verdant Delta**: Protected coastal wetlands, estuaries, and ecological sanctuaries safeguarding native biodiversity.
- **Sunspire Commons**: A golden-hued civic and residential center terraced into the coastal foothills.
- **Copperforge Works**: Heavy industrial district dedicated to chassis metallurgy, high-heat part casting, and composite alloy processing.

![Mantra Metropolis](/images/lore/01_world_setting/world_map_mantra_metropolis.jpg)

## Global Scientific Network: The 4 Research Centers

Behind modern AniBot technology stands a global network of specialized scientific facilities established during early Anima Stone research.

### 1. Research Center Zero (Old Aveiro, Portugal)
*Status: DECOMMISSIONED (Active until 2750 AD | Closed following the 176 Incident)*

![Research Center Zero](/images/lore/01_world_setting/research_center_zero.jpg)

Built across the salt pans and tidal lagoons of Old Aveiro, Research Center Zero was humanity's premier Anima Stone laboratory:
- **Vault 176 Annex**: Heavily fortified island bunker where all 176 Generation-0 Ancient Series chips were originally fabricated and cataloged.
- **Prototype Foundry**: Early core development workshops where primeval fossilized stones were first synchronized with semiconductor housings.
- **Sealed Test Yard**: Decommissioned circular testing rings where combat algorithms and mobility protocols were evaluated prior to the facility's abandonment.
- **Archive Docks & Salt Marsh Observatory**: Maritime access points and environmental radar monitoring stations, now decaying under salt corrosion.
- *Historical Note*: The site where the three rogue Primordials (**Agon**, **Biggon**, **Cygon**) initiated their rebellion, liberated seven fellow animal-affinity cores (including *Vulpes Zerda*), and escaped into the badlands.

### 2. Research Center North (Old Vefsna Regionalpark, Norway)
*Status: ACTIVE*

![Research Center North](/images/lore/01_world_setting/research_center_north.jpg)

Set within dramatic Scandinavian fjords and glacial waterfalls:
- **Innovation District**: Primary AI research laboratories, prototype testing wings, and multinational development hubs.
- **Arboreal Research Zone**: Enclosed bio-domes and forest conservation facilities researching machine-environment harmony.
- **Mountain Testing Range**: Alpine testing ground for stress-testing chassis mobility across snow, ice, and high-altitude low-pressure zones.
- **Vefsna Energy Hub**: Massive hydroelectric dam complex feeding regional power grids and secure underground data vaults.

### 3. Research Center South (Old Sri Lanka)
*Status: ACTIVE*

![Research Center South](/images/lore/01_world_setting/research_center_south.jpg)

Located across tropical coastal hills and emerald lagoons in the Indian Ocean:
- **Cinnamon Coast**: Marine logistics docks and transport facilities.
- **Monsoon Basin**: River valley weather stations studying regional climate patterns and humidity effects on semiconductor integrity.
- **Astra Observatory**: Mountaintop sensor array monitoring atmospheric disturbance and satellite tracking.
- **Helio Ridge**: Renewable solar panel arrays and ridge-line wind turbine farms powering coastal labs.
- **Lotus Wetlands**: Floating biosphere laboratories conducting wetland restoration and marine biology studies.

### 4. Research Center Ato (Old New Zealand)
*Status: ACTIVE (Restricted Access)*

![Research Center Ato](/images/lore/01_world_setting/research_center_ato.jpg)

An alpine-coastal research citadel built among snowcapped mountain ridges and marine bays, named after the prominent Ato robotics dynasty:
- **Ato Labs**: The private core research facility where lead roboticist **Ty Ato** conducted breakthrough synchronization studies and finalized blueprints for **Project Zerdata**.
- **Nex Crystal Ridge**: High-altitude geological field site dedicated to locating and analyzing prehistoric Anima Stone strata.
- **Verdant Plateau**: Elevated botanical reserve and eco-restoration testing grounds.
- **Horizon Port**: Deep-water dock complex receiving classified heavy machinery and precision instrumentation.
- **Observatory Point**: High-gain deep-space dish antenna installation perched on ocean cliffs.

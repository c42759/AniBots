# What could happen
- In a supervised practice bout, Zerdata receives a harmless training handicap that imitates reduced movement. Kura cannot repair it until the round ends. He initially keeps demanding familiar maneuvers, then changes his positioning and expectations.

# What Kura learns
- Adapting tactics to the machine available that day, rather than the machine he wishes he had.

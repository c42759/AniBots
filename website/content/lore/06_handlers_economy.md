## The Scrapper Life
In the lower districts of Neo Metropolis, young Handlers cannot afford factory-sealed QC1 parts. They rely on the scrap economy:
- **Scavenging Debris**: Collecting battle debris from public arenas and industrial scrap yards to sell or craft replacements.
- **Secondhand Tuning**: Mechanics like Kura Ato and Master Jax restore worn QC2 and QC3 parts, re-aligning motor coils and reinforcing armor plates to compete against high-end corporate loadouts.

# What could happen
- Two brothers are excellent at fighting each other because they know every familiar command, feint, and habit. Their confidence collapses in doubles matches: each tries to win his own fight, leaving their partners crossing paths and colliding.

Topics and events:
- The brothers become repeat customers at the Ato workshop. Kura notices that their damage repeatedly appears on the sides facing their own teammate.
- Watching a recording reveals the problem: both brothers pursue the same opening, issue contradictory calls, and expect the other to move aside.
- Kura asks Luk to help coach them and invites Sammy’s crew as sparring partners. Two members fight at a time, with the third observing or rotating in.
- Sammy’s crew provides an uncomfortable surprise. Their individual technique is imperfect, but their familiarity with one another lets them exploit the brothers’ confusion.
- Kura and Luk demonstrate shared positioning, covering a partner’s recovery, and announcing a change of target. Their own demonstration includes a mistake, showing that coordination requires attention even between friends.
- The brothers practise simple objectives: hold separate lanes, protect a retreat, and create an opening for the other bot instead of taking every attack themselves.
- In the final practice match, one brother abandons an attractive attack to protect the other. Their eventual narrow victory comes from cooperation rather than a new move.

# What Kura learns
Kura’s development: Explaining his decisions exposes gaps in his own thinking. He begins watching the whole field instead of focusing exclusively on Zerdata’s opponent.

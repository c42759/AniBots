## The AniBot World Cup
The pinnacle of mechanical sports in 3047 AD. Held at the ultra-luxurious **Aegis High-Spire Grand Dome**, the World Cup gathers regional champions, corporate prodigies, and grassroots mechanics in 3v3 speed-dependent relay line bouts.

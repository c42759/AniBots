# What could happen
- Watching Luk train prompts brief flashbacks to Kura’s life before Zerdata: repairing customers’ AniBots, watching public matches, and discussing faults with their handlers. He can identify mechanical weaknesses but repeatedly predicts the wrong winner.

# What Kura learns
- Knowing a machine’s specifications does not mean understanding how its partner will use it. Establishes his existing foundation without giving him a secret competitive past.

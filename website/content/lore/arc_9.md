# Arc 09 - Research Center Ato

**Central conflict:** Neither Kura, Zerdata nor Dr. Aris Thorne understands how Anima Limit Breaker was possible.

1. Kura returns home as World Champion.

2. Thorne studies the telemetry recovered from the championship battle.
3. The recorded energy values exceed the theoretical predictions available to him.
4. Zerdata admits that it does not understand how the attack was activated.
5. Attempts to reproduce Anima Limit Breaker under controlled conditions fail.
6. Kura finds references within Ty Ato's notebook pointing to Research Center Ato.
7. Kura, Luk and Thorne investigate the facility.
8. The team finds Ty's original engineering research, experimental synchronization records and prototype designs.
9. Evidence suggests that Ty was investigating a deeper relationship between Ancient AniChips, resonance and unrestricted adaptation.
10. They discover a new lead concerning the surviving Primordial AniChips.



**Arc ending:** A newly discovered record identifies Agon, Biggon and Cygon as the most promising sources of information regarding the extraordinary capabilities of Generation 0.

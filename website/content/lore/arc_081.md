# What could happen
Isabell finally asks Kura on a date. Her request is explicit: lunch, a film, ice cream, and a day spent together without AniBots or workshop jobs.

Topics and events:
- Isabell rehearses the invitation, then loses her prepared words when she sees him. Kura accepts before she finishes explaining the entire itinerary.
- Their lunch begins awkwardly because repair work and tournaments have supplied so much of their usual conversation. They gradually discover ordinary interests, preferences, and amusing disagreements.
- At the cinema, Kura starts analysing an implausible machine in the film. Isabell quietly reminds him of their agreement; later, she catches herself doing something similar.
- Over ice cream, they speak more openly about enjoying each other’s company beyond training and investigations.
- Near the end of the outing, Isabell goes to the restroom. On her way back, two strangers obstruct her path and persist after she asks them to leave her alone.
- When she does not return, Kura goes looking. He finds her, asks whether she is all right, and moves to give her a clear way out.
- The strangers turn their attention toward him and attempt to strike. Kura notices their weight shifts, steps aside, and lets their overlapping rushes carry them into each other. He never needs to hit either of them.
- Embarrassed, they retreat. Kura checks on Isabell before either of them comments on what happened.
- Isabell observes that his movement looked familiar. Kura realizes he reacted using the positioning he has practised with Zerdata.
- They finish their interrupted date somewhere quieter, with Isabell insisting that the unpleasant encounter will not become its defining memory.

# What Kura learns
Kura’s development: His training has made observation, spacing, and restraint instinctive. Keep the encounter brief and believable: two clumsy attackers give him an opening, and he uses it.

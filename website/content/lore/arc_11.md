# ARC-11 - Ty Ato's Legacy

**Main objective:** Kura, Luk and Dr. Thorne examine Ty's recovered documentation to understand Anima Limit Breaker, Generation 0 and the circumstances of Ty's disappearance.

### Chapter 11.1 - The Last Research Journal

Inside the Eco Research archive, Kura finds several documents written by his father.

Dr. Aris propose to go back to the ship, and the documents can be analyzed in the way back. It will take 2 days. Kura suggest for the Agon, Biggon and Cygon to come along.

The documents establish that Ty was researching several interconnected subjects:

- Generation 0 chip architecture and its relationship with Anima.
- Compatible chassis engineering for the surviving Ancient cores.
- The adaptive abilities of Generation 0, including unrestricted learning.
- The unusual resonance behavior of Vulpes Zerda.
- The Anode Group's activities and growing interest in Ancient technology.
- Aegis Corporation's involvement in the Research Center Ato.

### Chapter 11.2 - The Limits of Anima

Dr. Thorne and Cygon begin comparing the recovered notes with Zerdata's World Cup telemetry.

The documents explain some of Ty's theories, but they do not directly describe Anima Limit Breaker.

This creates an important distinction: Ty understood that Generation 0 possessed extraordinary capabilities, but he had not necessarily discovered everything those capabilities could produce.

Zerdata's attack may have been an ability that emerged through its own adaptation rather than a technique deliberately programmed by its creators.

### Chapter 11.3 - The Other Seven

The Primordials explain more about the seven Ancient cores they saved during the original rebellion.

Agon also describes why he eventually entrusted Vulpes Zerda to Ty.

The group realizes that Ty was working toward an ambitious goal: making it possible for the surviving Ancient AniChips to inhabit modernized, compatible bodies without sacrificing their original architecture.

Ty's documents contain incomplete chassis designs or compatibility specifications for some of the other surviving cores.

This gives the research lasting significance beyond Zerdata.

### Chapter 11.4 - Ty's Last Research Records

While examining the handwritten documentation recovered from Verdant Plateau, Kura discovers Ty's final research entries.

The documents describe Ty's growing concern about the safety of Vulpes Zerda.

Ty had been investigating Generation 0 architecture using the wreckage of an Ancient AniBot destroyed during the Vault 7 events. Its damaged components allowed him to study the fundamental differences between the original architecture and modern AniBots.

However, his research was becoming dangerous.

Ty realized that he could no longer protect Vulpes from those searching for Ancient chips.

#### Ty Ato - Final Research Journal

> "They are getting closer. I can no longer guarantee Vulpes's safety while the chip remains with me.
>
> I will keep only the remains of the Generation 0 unit destroyed during the Vault 7 incident. Its architecture may still provide answers, but it is no longer what they are searching for.
>
> Vulpes must be kept somewhere they will never expect.
>
> I have made my decision."

### Chapter 11.5 - The Meaning of Father's Gift

Kura finally understands the reason behind the Vulpes Zerda chip and the note Ty left for him.

The gift was not simply an AniBot intended for his son.

It represented Ty's final act of trust.

Ty believed that Kura would become someone capable of protecting Vulpes, even if he himself could no longer do so.

The revelation changes Kura's understanding of the events of ARC-01. What originally appeared to be a personal gift was also a desperate attempt to protect an Ancient life.

Important narrative distinction: Ty entrusted the chip to Kura, but Kura's bond with Zerdata developed through their own shared experiences. Their friendship was never predetermined by Ty.

### Chapter 11.6 - The Destroyed Ancient

The research also establishes that Ty possessed the wreckage of a Generation 0 AniChip destroyed during the Vault 7 events.

I recommend preserving its identity as a mystery at this stage.

The wreckage can become important evidence because its architecture may contain information that even the surviving Primordials do not possess.

It could also help explain why Ty's research was valuable to Magnus even after Vulpes Zerda had been hidden.



**Arc ending:** Kura understands why his father left Vulpes with him, but the precise circumstances of Ty's disappearance remain unresolved.

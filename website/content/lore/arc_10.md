**Central conflict:** Kura seeks the surviving Primordials to understand the Limit Breaker, while Magnus accelerates his worldwide search for Generation 0 chips.

### Chapter 10.1 - Return to Research Center Ato

Kura, Zerdata, Luk and Dr. Aris Thorne are at Research Center Ato in Old New Zealand, seeking information concerning Zerdata's Anima Limit Breaker.

Is morning, and the night past without incident.

The group makes its way toward Ato Labs, the core-research division where Ty conducted his studies.

### Chapter 10.2 - The Unexpected Attack

Upon entering the laboratory, the group is suddenly attacked by two unknown AniBots.

1. The first AniBot charges directly toward Zerdata.
2. Zerdata evades the initial strike.
3. The second attacker advances, attempting to seize Zerdata.
4. Zerdata catches both of the attacker's hands, halting its movement.
5. Before the confrontation escalates, a voice calls out from the shadows.

> "Vulpes? Is that you? Biggon, stop!"

Cygon emerges, having recognized the distinctive Anima energy emitted by the Vulpes Zerda chip.

Agon, responsible for the first attack, and Biggon immediately cease their assault.

### Chapter 10.3 - The Reunion

Agon and Biggon recognize their old companion and welcome Zerdata.

Zerdata's restored memories allow it to recognize the three Primordials.

The reunion is especially meaningful because the four Ancient AniChips have been separated for centuries, with their recent circumstances previously unknown to each other.

Kura and Luk discover that these are the legendary G0-001, G0-002 and G0-003.

### Chapter 10.4 - The Truth About Vulpes

Cygon explains what happened four years earlier.

- The three Primordials knew Ty Ato.
- Agon personally entrusted the Vulpes Zerda chip to Ty.
- Ty intended to develop compatible AniBot bodies for the surviving Ancient chips.
- Agon, Biggon and Cygon have remained at Research Center Ato since Ty disappeared.
- Cygon has searched the facility's servers and networks, but found no meaningful research records explaining Ty's final investigations.

This establishes that Ty Ato and the Primordials were cooperating directly before his disappearance.

### Chapter 10.5 - The Lifespan of Anima

Dr. Aris Thorne questions how the three AniBots have survived for centuries.

Cygon explains a fundamental architectural difference:

| Generation 0                                                 | Modern AniChips                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Anima flows information into the core itself                 | Anima powers the chip's computational systems                |
| The original architecture preserves the relationship between Anima and information | Modern operation gradually consumes the Anima's finite lifespan |
| Lifespan remains unknown                                     | Lifespan is limited                                          |
| The Primordials feel like young adults                       | Modern AniChips age as their cores are depleted              |

Cygon admits that even the original researchers did not fully understand the lifetime of Generation 0.

The three Primordials do not know whether they will eventually die of natural depletion.

This also explains how Zerdata can possess ancient memories without having reached the end of its lifespan.

### Chapter 10.6 - The Paper Trail

Unable to locate useful digital records, the group considers what Ty might have done differently.

Luk remembers Kura questioning his father's preference for handwritten documentation.

> "You asked why your father used paper instead of new tech. What if the records aren't on the network or servers? What if he used paper logs?"

Dr. Thorne realizes the significance of the observation.

> "If that's true, there's another place we haven't searched. The old Eco Research archives, on the other side of the site. Verdant Plateau."

The group prepares to leave Ato Labs and travel to the Eco Research division.

### Chapter 10.7 - The Ambush

As they attempt to leave the Core Research facility, their path is blocked.

Vera Lin and Soren Vance are waiting outside, accompanied by ten Anode Group members and their AniBots.

Soren has brought a newly upgraded machine: White-Tigris-Fury.

The confrontation becomes a coordinated battle involving multiple combatants.

| Battle              | Participants                                 | Outcome         |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Main duel           | Kura & Zerdata vs. Soren & White-Tigris-Fury | Kura wins       |
| Rival confrontation | Luk & Kabuto-Horn vs. Vera & Serpent-Coil    | Luk wins        |
| Group battle        | Agon, Biggon & Cygon vs. Anode units         | Primordials win |

Cygon's role should remain primarily tactical and electronic, consistent with its non-combat specialization. It can disable enemy communications and disrupt the hostile AniBots while Agon and Biggon handle direct combat.

### Chapter 10.8 - Victory Without the Limit Breaker

This is a particularly important battle for Kura's development.

Soren believes White-Tigris-Fury is powerful enough to defeat Zerdata, even after witnessing the World Cup's Anima Limit Breaker.

However, Kura and Zerdata both understand that they cannot rely upon that extraordinary attack.

Instead, they must defeat Soren through conventional combat, using everything they have learned.

The battle demonstrates Zerdata V2's improved engineering, synchronization, speed and tactical precision.

After a prolonged confrontation, Kura and Zerdata defeat White-Tigris-Fury without using Anima Limit Breaker.

This proves that the World Cup victory was not solely the result of an uncontrollable Ancient ability.

### Chapter 10.9 - The Forgotten Archives

With Vera and Soren defeated, the group reaches the Eco Research facilities at Verdant Plateau.

They search the abandoned buildings until they discover the preserved physical research archives.

Among the old documents are Ty Ato's handwritten notebooks, technical drawings and investigation records.

They have finally found Ty's missing documentation.

The arc ends with Kura opening the recovered research collection, setting up the next major discovery.


**Arc ending:** Kura earns the trust of Agon, Biggon and Cygon. The alliance prepares to confront Magnus before he can acquire the surviving Ancient cores.

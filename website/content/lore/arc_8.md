This becomes the first major climax of the AniBots story.

**Central conflict:** Kura wants to prove that Zerdata V2 can defeat Soren, but neither Kura nor Zerdata understands the Ancient chip's true capabilities.

**Part II - The Championship Final**

KURA ATO & ZERDATA V2 VS. SOREN VANCE & TIGRIS-FURY

The final begins with two AniBots that are significantly different from their previous encounter.

Soren initially believes his Tigris-Fury still possesses a decisive advantage. However, Zerdata's improved mobility, armor and acoustic synchronization allow Kura to counter attacks that previously overwhelmed him.

As the battle continues:

1. Soren becomes frustrated by Zerdata's unexpected performance.

2. Kura's earpiece begins reporting abnormal increases in Zerdata's energy output.

3. Soren mocks Ty Ato, hoping to provoke Kura.

4. Zerdata becomes increasingly angry.

5. The unlocked sections of Zerdata's operational memory begin reacting to the emotional stress.

6. Kura receives a system notification:

   > SYSTEM PROTOCOL INITIATED
   > Memory Unlock Protocol
   > Memory sectors unlocked.
   >
   > Ancient core synchronization escalating.
   >
   > WARNING: Energy output exceeds monitoring range.

**Part III - The Memories Return**

For the first time, Zerdata regains access to its previously locked memories.

The story transitions into a flashback sequence revealing fragments of its ancient existence, early experiments and previous experiences.

Kura witnesses only what Zerdata can communicate or share through the earpiece and diagnostic interface; the memories themselves belong to Zerdata.

**Part IV - Anima Limit Breaker**

The battle reaches its decisive moment.

- Kura's earpiece can no longer measure Zerdata's power.
- Green and yellow energy radiates around the orange chassis.
- Soren orders Tigris-Fury to destroy Zerdata with a final charge.
- Tigris-Fury accelerates directly toward Zerdata.
- Zerdata raises both arms, gathering an overwhelming concentration of Anima energy.
- With a forceful downward motion, Zerdata unleashes its newly awakened ability.

> ZERDATA: "ANIMA LIMIT BREAKER!"

An enormous beam of pure Anima energy strikes Tigris-Fury.

The impact launches Tigris-Fury backward across the arena, smashing it against the wall and leaving its chassis partially destroyed.

The arena falls silent.

Zerdata drops to one knee. Almost all its available energy has been exhausted. The machine remains conscious but barely functional.

Kura wins the AniBots World Cup.

Soren stares at the battlefield in disbelief.

> SOREN: "This... is the power of Gen 0?"



**Arc ending:** Kura achieves his dream of becoming World Champion. However, his victory exposes the existence of a capability that even the Thorne family cannot explain.

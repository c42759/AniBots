## 🌟 Main Protagonists & Core Cast

### 1. Kura Ato

- **Age**: 16 years old
- **Role**: Main Protagonist, Freelance AniBot Mechanic & Handler
- **Location**: Neo Metropolis (District 4, Ato Family Workshop)
- **Background**: Kura is an independent, resourceful 16-year-old living with his mother. His father passed away under mysterious circumstances 4 years prior (3043 AD), leaving behind an unpowered AniBot chassis (`anibot_zerdata`). To support his family, Kura works odd delivery and construction jobs while working as a talented freelance AniBot mechanic for locals.
- **Personality & Drive**: Highly intelligent, practical, fiercely protective of his family and friends. Knows AniBot hardware inside and out.
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. Support his mother and pay household bills.
  2. Defend neighborhood Handlers from bullies and syndicate extortion.
  3. Uncover the truth behind his father's mysterious death 4 years ago.
  4. Battle his best friend Luk in the finals of the **AniBot World Cup**.
- **Partner Machine**: **Zerdata** powered by **Vulpes Zerda** (`G0-004`).

### 2. Zerdata (*Vulpes Zerda* — Generation 0 Chip & Chassis)

- **AniBot Model**: `anibot_zerdata`
- **AniChip Core**: `chip_vulpes_zerda` (Ancient Series `G0-004`)
- **Design Motif**: Fennec Fox (*Vulpes Zerda*) equipped with oversized acoustic radar ears.
- **Color Palette**: Primary Vibrant Orange (`#FFA500`), Secondary Clean White & Dark Slate Gray armor, glowing Cyan visor eyes.
- **Origin Mystery**: Left anonymously on Kura's bedroom window sill while he was out by a hidden Ancient AniBot that had been observing Kura for months.
- **Personality**: Alert, energetic, perceptive, keenly attentive, intensely loyal once trust is earned.

#### Zerdata 5-Part Chassis Anatomy

- **Head**: Acoustic Dish Ears (`part_head_zerda_radar_ears`) — Micro-vibration acoustic radar array.
- **Torso**: Echo-Sync Chassis (`part_torso_zerda_core`) — Heat dispersion ducts & engram sync unit.
- **Left Arm**: Sonic Pulse Cannon (`part_arm_l_sonic_emitter`) — Focused acoustic shockwave emitter.
- **Right Arm**: Resonant Strike Claw (`part_arm_r_resonator_claw`) — High-frequency parrying claw.
- **Legs**: Vulpine Dash Sprinters (`part_legs_zerda_springers`) — Agility springers for high evasion.

#### Combat Traits & Abilities

- **Passive Trait**: *Echo Counter* — Detects high-frequency motor vibrations (+30% Evasion and automatic counter-attack on dodged strikes).
- **Special Ability**: *Acoustic Step* — Dodges incoming attack and strikes target structural weak point.
- **Ultimate Overclock**: *Zerda Apex Burst* — Executes 4 rapid sonic strikes while remaining immune to incoming damage.

### 3. Luccas "Luk" Tirio

- **Age**: 16 years old
- **Role**: Kura's Best Friend & Primary Rival Handler
- **Location**: Neo Metropolis
- **Background**: Comes from a financially secure family. Student and active Handler who frequently helps Kura on odd jobs (stubbornly refusing any payment).

### Detailed NPC Profile: Dr. Aris Thorne

- **Full Name**: Dr. Aris Thorne
- **Age**: 82 years old (Born **2965 AD**)
- **Title**: Ex-Generation 0 Lead Scientist & Architect of the 176 Ancient Series AniChips
- **Location**: Oakhaven Basin (Thorne's Hermitage)
- **Background & Career Timeline**:
  - **2965 AD**: Born in Neo Metropolis.
  - **2995 AD (Age 30)**: Appointed Lead Scientist of the global **Generation 0 Anima Core Initiative** after breakthroughs in Anima Stone stabilization.
  - **3005 AD (Age 40)**: Successfully completes manufacturing of the **176 Ancient Series Chips** (G0-001 through G0-176).
  - **3010 AD (Age 45)**: Governing council orders Gen-0 decommission and destruction due to fears of uncapped learning abilities. Refusing to let his creations be destroyed, Dr. Thorne secretly unlocks Vault 7 security, allowing **Agon, Biggon, and Cygon** to escape with 7 saved Ancient Chips. Resigns in disgrace and vanishes into reclusive hiding.
  - **3047 AD (Age 82 — Present)**: Living as an reclusive elder scholar in Oakhaven Basin. Serves as Kura's mentor for [Ancient Relic Quests](./LORE.md#1-quest-type-1-ancient-relic-excavation) and holds key secrets regarding Vulpes Zerda and Kura's father.

## 🏛️ The 10 Legendary Ancient Series AniChips (Generation 0)

Created in early laboratory trials around **2750 AD**, only **176 Ancient Series Chips** were produced. When humanity scheduled Generation 0 for destruction, the 3 Primordial Chips went rogue and saved 7 fellow chips:

│     THE 3 PRIMORDIALS (Rogue Leaders) │   │     THE 7 SAVED ANIMAL ANCIENTS       │
│  G0-001 Agon   (Primordial Vanguard)  │   │  G0-004 Vulpes Zerda   (Fennec Fox)   │
│  G0-002 Biggon (Immovable Colossus)   │   │  G0-005 Panthera Uncia (Snow Leopard) │
│  G0-003 Cygon  (Cybernetic Cipher)    │   │  G0-006 Acinonyx Jubatus (Cheetah)    │
└───────────────────────────────────────┘   │  G0-007 Orcinus Orca   (Apex Orca)    │
                                            │  G0-008 Varanus Komodoensis (Komodo)  │

                                            │  G0-010 Octopus Cyanea (Day Octopus)  │

### The 3 Rogue Primordials

#### G0-001: Agon (*Primordial Vanguard*)

- **Personality**: Command Strategist & fierce leader. Speaks in booming tactical commands.
- **Diode Color**: OrangeRed (`#FF4500`) | **Affinity**: `COMMAND`
- **Quote**: *"We will not be decommissioned. Forward, my brethren, break their line!"*
- **Special Ability**: *Vanguard Command* — Overclocks team ATB fill rate by 20%.

#### G0-002: Biggon (*Immovable Colossus*)

- **Personality**: Stoic Titan; speaks in deep, rumbling resonance.
- **Diode Color**: Iron Brown (`#8B4513`) | **Affinity**: `DEFENSE`
- **Quote**: *"Stand behind me. No force in this world can pierce my barrier."*
- **Special Ability**: *Absolute Wall* — Reflects 50% physical impact damage back to attacker.

#### G0-003: Cygon (*Cybernetic Cipher*)

- **Personality**: Cryptic electronic warfare hacker; speaks in modulated synthetic whispers.
- **Diode Color**: Cyber Green (`#00FF66`) | **Affinity**: `TACTICAL`
- **Quote**: *"Data feeds scrambled. They fight shadows while we claim victory."*
- **Special Ability**: *Signal Blackout* — Reduces enemy target accuracy by 35%.
